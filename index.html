    Work

    Taking a new product from idea to launch in 13 months

    Configuration & Change Management started as a gap I kept hearing about from operators and in RFPs: no single place to manage network changes, audit them and prove compliance. I built the case, chose the foundations that had to ship first, and made AI agents part of the product from day one, with access controls on every agent action. It launched in 13 months with four agents in production.

    Building product security where none existed

    When I took on product security in 2021, the portfolio had no secure development process. I built one on Microsoft SDL, OWASP and NIST guidance, made security sign-off a release gate, and automated the scanning, SBOMs and vulnerability reporting that used to be manual. It now covers seven products.

    Rebuilding identity on open standards

    Authentication had grown product by product. I moved it onto OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, SAML and JWT, including machine-to-machine access, so customers connect their own identity providers without custom work.
